Mix cash and card

Carry some local cash for taxis and small shops, and a forex card for the rest. It's safer than carrying all cash.

Tell your bank

Turn on international use for your debit or credit card before you leave, so payments abroad aren't blocked.

Always pay in local currency

If a card machine offers to charge you in rupees, say no. Paying in the local currency is usually cheaper.

Check visa rules officially

Visa and entry rules change often. Confirm them on the country's official website or the Indian embassy page before you book.
