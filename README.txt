TripKhojo – clickable prototype (for internal testing only)

HOW TO OPEN
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser (Chrome recommended).
3. Use the links in the page to move between screens.

PAGES
- index.html  – The Journey Engine: living globe, Budget Magic, Surprise Me
- home.html   – Immersive homepage with destinations and trending
- tools.html  – Budget finder, AI planner (demo), fare calendar
- trip.html   – Sample trip page: Rishikesh weekend

NOTES
- Needs an internet connection only for the fonts; everything else works offline.
- All prices, fares and times are indicative sample estimates, not live prices.
- Search, booking, save/share and alerts are demonstrations only.
- Destination pictures are illustrations; real photos come before launch.
